About the Dataset

This dataset consists of 10 popular modern video games, containing their core technical and commercial attributes, along with their platform releases, release dates, and Metacritic scores.

Attributes

  • id (Integer): Unique identifier for each game.
  • title (String): Official title of the video game.
  • publisher (String): Company that published the game.
  • developer (String): Studio that developed the game.
  • genre (String): Main gameplay genre.
  • launch_price_usd (Decimal): Launch retail price in USD.
  • pegi_rating (Integer): Age rating by PEGI (3, 7, 12, 16, 18).
  • game_engine (String): Engine used to develop the game.
  • multiplayer (Boolean): Whether the game has multiplayer (true/false).
  • platform_name / name (String): Platform the game was released on.
  • release_date (Date): Release date on that platform (YYYY-MM-DD).
  • metacritic_score (Integer, nullable): Metacritic review score (0-100).
